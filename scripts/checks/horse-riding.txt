# terrain, far from the shared spawn floor x 3..16, z 2..16, y 60..70, and from the boat

#                                    the tamed, saddled horse 3 blocks ahead (the saddle is
#                                    the SADDLE entry of the `equipment` map in 26.3's NBT —
#                                    `SaddleItem` is the pre-datafix name and is ignored;
#                                    NoAI: a free horse wanders off its mounting spot while
#                                    the script's first wait runs, and the ridden travel is
#                                    unaffected)

# EVIDENCE (RCON, own username riding-mount-hud, on 25691; see horse-riding.sh for the reads):
#   the horse's Pos before the ride (mounted, at rest) and after (W 3 s and a charged

#   no "moved wrongly"/"moved too quickly" for the horse.

# its body), hold W for 3 s, then hold Space briefly (charging the jump bar) and release
