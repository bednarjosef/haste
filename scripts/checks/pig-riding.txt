# Pig riding with a carrot on a stick on the test server (127.0.0.1:25691). Live evidence and
# the steps that drive the client; the RCON setup the driver runs is in pig-riding.sh.
#
# RIG (this branch's own area, x 400..420, z 0..20, like the horse platform):
#   fill 400 71 0 420 90 20 air      clear above the platform first (the natural terrain is
#                                    higher than y 71 and would bury the player)
#   fill 400 70 0 420 70 20 stone    platform, top at y 71
#   tp <name> 405.5 71 5.0 0 20      the player at the platform's edge (eyes y 72.62)
#   give <name> carrot_on_a_stick    the steer stick, in hotbar slot 0
#   summon minecraft:pig 405.5 71 8.5 {NoAI:1b,equipment:{saddle:{...}}}
#                                    the saddled pig 3 blocks ahead (NoAI: a free pig wanders
#                                    off its mounting spot; the ridden travel is unaffected)
#
# EVIDENCE (RCON, own username PigCheck, on 25691; see pig-riding.sh for the reads):
#   the pig's Pos before the ride (mounted, at rest) and after (the walk plus a boost), the
#   rider's RootVehicle naming the pig, and the server log's verdict — no "moved wrongly"/
#   "moved too quickly" for it.
#
# The steps below drive the client: mount the pig with use-item (the crosshair aims at its
# body), then a second use click while holding the carrot on a stick starts the pig's boost
# (vanilla `FoodOnAStickItem.use` -> `ItemSteerable.boost`), and the pig walks forward on its
# own from then (vanilla `Pig.getRiddenInput` is a constant full forward while the rider
# holds the stick). The first `wait 600` is generous on purpose: the offscreen client's
# ticks run slow while the world settles, so the click lands well after the RCON setup.
wait_until joined
wait 600
look 0 20
wait 10
click right 480 270
wait 40
click right 480 270
wait 400
