# Feature: item data drives breaking and attacking like vanilla. The held stack's
# minecraft:tool component (with the item defaults), the Efficiency enchantment's
# mining_efficiency, the attribute modifiers' attack_speed and the consumable's use
# duration all come from the server's item data; this script holds attack on a floating
# block the runner places over RCON, and the log's "finished breaking ... after N
# destroy ticks" lines are compared with vanilla's tick counts. The runner swaps the
# main hand's item between cases while we look at the sky.
#
# Geometry: we stand at (145.5, 71, 4.5) on a stone floor, the target block floats at
# (145, 72, 6) — one block above the floor line, air on every side along the ray, so
# once it breaks the held attack hits nothing (out of the 4.5-block reach) and no
# stray break starts. Eye at y 72.62, target centre (145.5, 72.5, 6.5): pitch 4.
