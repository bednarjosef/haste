# Container check: crafting table. The crafting screen is 176x166 centred in the 480x270
# GUI, so its origin is (152, 52) and a slot's centre is its menu position + 8 (vanilla
# CraftingMenu: result (124,35), grid (30,17) step 18, inventory (8,84), hotbar (8,142)).
#   result (menu slot 0)          -> (152+124+8, 52+35+8)  = (284, 95)
#   grid (menu slots 1-9)        -> (152+30+8, 52+17+8)   = (190, 77), step 18
#   player inventory (slots 10+) -> (152+8+8, 52+84+8)    = (168, 144), step 18
#   hotbar (slots 37-45)         -> (152+8+8, 52+142+8)   = (168, 202), step 18
# The runner starts a fresh player (always alive, empty, at the world spawn), then tps it
# into this check's own area (x 120..130, z 0..10: never the shared spawn, where other
# workers' players wander through the crosshair), gives the items and places the table
# when the setup screenshot appears.
wait_until joined
wait_until settled
screenshot ccraft-setup
# The runner now tps us to (125.5, 71, 2.5) facing the table, clears the inventory,
# gives the items and places the table. Waiting for the hotbar (not a fixed delay)
# absorbs the busy shared server: the give's inventory sync can lag its RCON answer
# by many seconds.
wait_until hotbar_item
# Open the table: look at it and use (the runner placed it at (125, 71, 5), on the
# plain's ground so its north face is visible from the camera; from the eye at (125.5,
# 72.62, 2.5), pitch 20 aims at the face at y ~71.7). The look is re-aimed right before
# the use: the busy shared server sends correction teleports after the RCON tp, and one
# landing between the aim and the click re-faces us with its (stale) rotation, so the
# click would miss — a player re-aims before clicking.
look 0 20
wait 5
look 0 20
mouse_down right
wait 2
mouse_up right
wait_until screen_open
wait 10
screenshot ccraft-open

# x = 190+18, y = 77+18 = (208, 95)).
