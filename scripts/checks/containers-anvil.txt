# Container check: anvil. The anvil screen is 176x166 centred in the 480x270 GUI, so its
# origin is (152, 52) and a slot's centre is its menu position + 8 (vanilla AnvilMenu:
# input (27,47), ingredient (76,47), result (134,47); the player inventory and hotbar are
# the container screen's standard rows).
#   input (menu slot 0)    -> (152+27+8, 52+47+8)  = (187, 107)
#   ingredient (slot 1)   -> (152+76+8, 52+47+8)  = (236, 107)
#   result (menu slot 2)  -> (152+134+8, 52+47+8) = (294, 107)
#   player inventory (slots 3+) -> (152+8+8, 52+84+8) = (168, 144), step 18
#   hotbar (slots 30+)    -> (152+8+8, 52+142+8)  = (168, 202), step 18
# The runner tps us into the check's own area, gives a wooden sword and one XP level, and
# places the anvil when the setup screenshot appears. The rename costs 1 level.
wait_until joined
wait_until settled
screenshot canvil-setup
# The runner now tps us to (122.5, 71, 2.5) facing the anvil, clears the inventory, gives
# the sword and the level. Waiting for the hotbar absorbs the busy shared server.
wait_until hotbar_item
# Open the anvil: look at it and use (the runner placed it at (122, 71, 5), on this
# check's own stone platform; from the eye at (122.5, 72.62, 2.5), pitch 20 aims at the
# face at y ~71.7).
look 0 20
wait 5
mouse_down right
wait 2
mouse_up right
wait_until screen_open
wait 10
screenshot canvil-open
# The sword goes into the input (slot 0), from hotbar slot 0.
click left 168 202
click left 187 107
# The anvil's name field is focused on open (vanilla focuses it in init): type the new
# name. The rename costs 1 level and fills the result.
type RenamedSword
wait 10
screenshot canvil-named
# Take the result: the renamed sword, shift-click straight into the inventory.
key_down LShift
wait 2
click left 294 107
wait 2
key_up LShift
wait 10
screenshot canvil-taken
# Close with E (the inventory key, vanilla's keyInventory path in keyPressed).
key E
wait 20
wait_until screen_closed
screenshot canvil-closed
# 10 s tail: the runner reads `data get entity ... Inventory` (the sword's custom name)
# from this frame on.
wait 200
