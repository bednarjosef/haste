# Container check: brewing stand. The brewing screen is 176x166 centred in the 480x270 GUI,
# so its origin is (152, 52) and a slot's centre is its menu position + 8 (vanilla
# BrewingStandMenu: potions (56,51) (79,58) (102,51), ingredient (79,17), fuel (17,17); the
# player inventory and hotbar are the container screen's standard rows).
#   potion slot 0 (menu slot 0) -> (152+56+8, 52+51+8)  = (216, 111)
#   potion slot 1 (menu slot 1) -> (152+79+8, 52+58+8)  = (239, 118)
#   potion slot 2 (menu slot 2) -> (152+102+8, 52+51+8) = (262, 111)
#   ingredient (menu slot 3)    -> (152+79+8, 52+17+8)  = (239, 77)
#   fuel (menu slot 4)          -> (152+17+8, 52+17+8)  = (177, 77)
#   player inventory (slots 5+) -> (152+8+8, 52+84+8)   = (168, 144), step 18
#   hotbar (slots 32+)          -> (152+8+8, 52+142+8) = (168, 202), step 18
# The runner tps us into the check's own area, gives a water bottle (a potion with water
# contents), a nether wart and a blaze powder and places the stand when the setup
# screenshot appears. The brew (water bottle + nether wart, fueled by the blaze powder)
# is vanilla's awkward potion and takes 400 ticks, waited out before the result is taken.
wait_until joined
wait_until settled
screenshot cbrew-setup
# The runner now tps us to (126.5, 71, 2.5) facing the stand, clears the inventory and gives
# the items. Waiting for the hotbar absorbs the busy shared server.
wait_until hotbar_item
# Open the stand: look at it and use (the runner placed it at (126, 71, 5), on this check's
# own stone platform; from the eye at (126.5, 72.62, 2.5), pitch 20 aims at the face at
# y ~71.7).
look 0 20
wait 5
mouse_down right
wait 2
mouse_up right
wait_until screen_open
wait 10
screenshot cbrew-open
# The water bottle goes into potion slot 0, the nether wart into the ingredient slot, the
# blaze powder into the fuel slot: pick each up from its own hotbar slot (0: 168 202,
# 1: 186 202, 2: 204 202) and drop it in.
click left 168 202
click left 216 111
click left 186 202
click left 239 77
click left 204 202
click left 177 77
# The brew takes 400 ticks (20 s) once the fuel burns; wait for the potion. The screen stays
# open (the vanilla client keeps it open while the arrow fills).
wait 600
screenshot cbrew-brewed
# Take the awkward potion: shift-click potion slot 0 straight into the inventory.
key_down LShift
wait 2
click left 216 111
wait 2
key_up LShift
wait 10
screenshot cbrew-taken
# Close with E (the inventory key, vanilla's keyInventory path in keyPressed).
key E
wait 20
wait_until screen_closed
screenshot cbrew-closed
# 10 s tail: the runner reads `data get entity ... Inventory` (the awkward potion) from
# this frame on.
wait 200
