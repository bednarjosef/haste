# Container check: furnace. The furnace screen is 176x166 centred in the 480x270 GUI, so
# its origin is (152, 52) and a slot's centre is its menu position + 8 (vanilla
# AbstractFurnaceMenu: input (56,17), fuel (56,53), result (116,35); the player inventory
# and hotbar are the container screen's standard rows).
#   input (menu slot 0)        -> (152+56+8, 52+17+8)  = (216, 77)
#   fuel (menu slot 1)         -> (152+56+8, 52+53+8)  = (216, 113)
#   result (menu slot 2)       -> (152+116+8, 52+35+8) = (276, 95)
#   player inventory (slots 3+) -> (152+8+8, 52+84+8)  = (168, 144), step 18
#   hotbar (slots 30+)         -> (152+8+8, 52+142+8)  = (168, 202), step 18
# The runner tps us into the check's own area, gives 1 raw iron and 1 coal and places the
# furnace when the setup screenshot appears; the smelt (1 raw iron, 200 ticks at full
# heat) is waited out before the result is taken.
wait_until joined
wait_until settled
screenshot cfurn-setup
# The runner now tps us to (128.5, 71, 2.5) facing the furnace, clears the inventory and
# gives the items. Waiting for the hotbar absorbs the busy shared server: the give's
# inventory sync can lag its RCON answer by many seconds.
wait_until hotbar_item
# Open the furnace: look at it and use (the runner placed it at (128, 71, 5), on this
# check's own stone platform; from the eye at (128.5, 72.62, 2.5), pitch 20 aims at the
# face at y ~71.7).
look 0 20
wait 5
mouse_down right
wait 2
mouse_up right
wait_until screen_open
wait 10
screenshot cfurn-open
# The raw iron goes into the input (slot 0), the coal into the fuel slot (slot 1): pick
# each up from its own hotbar slot (0: 168 202, 1: 186 202) and drop it in.
click left 168 202
click left 216 77
click left 186 202
click left 216 113
# The smelt takes 200 ticks (10 s) once the fuel burns; wait for the result. The furnace
# screen stays open (the vanilla client keeps it open while the arrow fills).
wait 260
screenshot cfurn-smelted
# Take the result: one iron ingot, shift-click straight into the inventory.
key_down LShift
wait 2
click left 276 95
wait 2
key_up LShift
wait 10
screenshot cfurn-taken
# Close with E (the inventory key, vanilla's keyInventory path in keyPressed).
key E
wait 20
wait_until screen_closed
screenshot cfurn-closed
# 10 s tail: the runner reads `data get entity ... Inventory` from this frame on, and
# the shared test server can take seconds to answer.
wait 200
